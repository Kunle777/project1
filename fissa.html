Support the School Masjid - Give Fī Sabīlillāh

Fī Sabīlillāh is an Arabic phrase meaning "in the way of Allah." It refers to charitable acts done solely for the pleasure of Allah, without expecting anything in return. One of the greatest ways to contribute Fī Sabīlillāh is by supporting the construction and maintenance of a masjid.

Crescent University’s school masjid serves as a place of worship, learning, and community gathering for students and staff. However, it requires expansion and maintenance to accommodate the growing number of worshippers.

By donating, you are earning continuous rewards (Sadaqah Jariyah) as every prayer and act of worship performed in the masjid will bring you blessings. Your contribution will help with renovations, additional prayer spaces, and providing essential resources for worshippers.

The Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) said: “Whoever builds a masjid for Allah, Allah will build for him a house in Paradise.” (Sahih al-Bukhari)

May Allah reward you abundantly for your generosity.
