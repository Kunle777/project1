IMASA CUAB Sponsorship Program

The Islamic Mission for Africa Student Association (IMASA) - Crescent University, Abeokuta (CUAB) chapter is dedicated to supporting students and providing healthcare assistance to communities in need. Our sponsorship program focuses on funding students who require financial assistance, supporting free medical outreach programs, and providing necessary health resources to underserved communities. Your donation can help cover tuition fees, purchase medical supplies, and fund community health initiatives. Every contribution, no matter how small, makes a significant impact.

Join us in making a difference by sponsoring a student or contributing to our cause. Your generosity will change lives and promote better healthcare for all.
