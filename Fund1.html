About Us

The Crescent University Fundraising System is dedicated to supporting the educational and infrastructural development of Crescent University, Abeokuta.

Our mission is to connect donors, alumni, and well-wishers with meaningful causes that improve student experiences and campus facilities.

Through our transparent and secure platform, we enable contributors to support various initiatives, including scholarships, infrastructure development, student welfare, and research grants.

By fostering a community of giving, we strive to create a lasting impact that benefits both current and future students of Crescent University.

Our Services

We offer a seamless, secure, and transparent platform to connect donors with meaningful causes at Crescent University.

  • Scholarship Funding: Supporting students through financial aid.
  • Infrastructure Development: Improving campus facilities and resources.
  • Fissabillulah: Donating to the Masjid.
  • Research Grants: Encouraging innovation and academic excellence.
